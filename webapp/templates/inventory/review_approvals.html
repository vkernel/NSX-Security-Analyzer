Single approval: the assigned owner reviews the finding. Approval moves it directly to Ready for decommissioning.

Two-person approval (default): the owner approves first, then a different operator or administrator must independently approve.

Saving also updates existing open reviews. If the requirement changes, prior approvals are retained in audit history and the review returns to its owner for a new decision. Rejected reviews stay rejected; completed decommissioning records remain unchanged. Evidence checks, a completion ticket and audit history remain required in both modes. The application never deletes NSX objects.
