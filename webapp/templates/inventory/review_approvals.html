Permissions and combined cleanup policies

Execution confirmation remains separate from approval and always requires NSX deletion permission. Choose requester only (default), either valid approver, or requester/approver. For firewall reference cleanup, approvers must have approved the exact cleanup plan; group and firewall-rule execution policies both apply. Execution-policy changes do not reset review approvals, but eligibility is checked again before each mutation.

How approvals work

Single approval: the assigned owner approves. Two-person approval (default): a different operator or administrator must also approve independently.

Evidence checks, a completion ticket and audit history remain required. NSX deletion requires a separate administrator preview and explicit confirmation.

Changing an approval-count setting returns affected open reviews to their owner for a new decision. Prior approvals remain in audit history. Rejected and completed reviews stay unchanged.
