How corrections are verified

Reviewed component values supply naming evidence without modifying group membership. Static member tags are suggestions only. Changing a component resets its review state.

Awaiting verification becomes Resolved only after a later collection confirms the expected name, unchanged definition and policy, and complete evidence.
